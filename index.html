📸How to Use the FLIR Here
Scan slowly for temperature breaks — edges where warm meets cool. Fish stack on those lines.

Best targets:
• Channel edges — deeper water stays cooler, fish the drop
• Grass flat margins — shaded patches run 2–4° cooler
• Inlet mouths — tidal water often a different temp
• Shaded cuts — north side of Horn Island in summer

Rule: The live NOAA reading above is your baseline. Your FLIR finds the cooler pockets within it — that's where the fish are.

💡 After Heavy Rain — Critical Rule
Pascagoula River dumps freshwater into the Sound after rain — salinity near the river mouth can crash to near-zero. Always check before committing. Under 10 PPT? Run east toward Horn Island. Specks return in 2–3 days once it stabilizes.

HORN ISLAND · SPECIES & HOW TO READ THEM
🐟Speckled Trout● HOT
FLIR target: 68–76°F. Above 80°, push to channel drop-offs.
Salinity: 15–25 PPT.
Best: First 90 min of daylight, again at last light.
Bait: Live shrimp under popping cork, 1/4 oz gold spoon, Gulp! on jig head.
🐠Redfish● HOT
FLIR trick: Reds tolerate 80–86°F — if specks went deep, switch to reds.
East end surf at first light. Reds up to 40–50 lbs work the break.
Bait: Gold spoon, live blue crab chunk, large Gulp! on the bottom.
🦈Flounder● ACTIVE
Ambush predators — you go to them. FLIR tip: scan sandy patches, sometimes show as a cooler outline.
Bait: Bucktail jig, curly tail, live finger mullet. Slow on the bottom.
🐡Tripletail● SIGHT FISH
April–October. Every crab trap float and channel marker. Pitch a live shrimp 3–4 ft ahead, drift natural. Don't twitch.
Salinity: 20–30 PPT.
🐟Spanish Mackerel● ACTIVE
Clear water + 25+ PPT = mackerel in. Watch for diving birds.
Bait: Small silver spoons, fast retrieve.
🦀Cobia● APR–OCT
Up to 100 lbs. Chum near buoys and structure. Look for them near the surface.
Bait: Live eel, live croaker, heavy bucktail jig.
PROVEN RIGS · HORN ISLAND
🪝 #1 — Popping Cork (Speck & Red)
Setup: Popping cork → 18–24" fluoro leader → 1/8 oz jig head → live shrimp or Gulp!
When: Incoming tide, grass flats, 2–4 ft. Pop every 3–4 sec — specks hit on the pause.
FLIR first: Above 82°? Move to channel edge.
🪝 #2 — Carolina Bottom Rig (Reds & Flounder)
Setup: 1 oz egg sinker → swivel → 18" 20lb fluoro → circle hook #3/0 → live shrimp or crab chunk
When: Surf east end, channel bottom. Cast, let it sit. Don't yank — circle hook sets itself. Works all day.
🪝 #3 — Free-lined Shrimp (Tripletail)
Setup: No weight. 2/0 hook through horn of shrimp. 20lb mono.
When: Sight fishing floats. Pitch 3–4 ft ahead. Let it sink natural. Do not twitch.
Salinity: Only worth it at 20+ PPT.
🪝 #4 — Gold Spoon (Surf Reds & Mackerel)
Setup: 1/2 oz Johnson Silver Minnow gold → 18" 25lb fluoro
Reds: Cast parallel to beach, slow steady retrieve.
Mackerel: Cast far, fast retrieve just under surface. Watch for birds.
KEY LOCATIONS · GAUTIER TO HORN ISLAND
🏠Dan's Dock → Horn Island~18 mi · 45 min
800 Roys Rd, Gautier — Pascagoula River system, head south through Back Bay, cross Sound southeast.

⚠️ Check wind before leaving. SSE under 12 knots = comfortable. 15+ = rough. Head back by 11am if afternoon wind builds.
🐠East End — Horn Island SurfBig Reds
Prime surf spot. Wade the beach at first light. Redfish to 50 lbs work the break in summer.

FLIR move: Scan the surf line for cooler pockets where deeper water surges in.

Also: pompano, sharks, large flounder, surface mackerel in clear water.
🌿North Side Flats — Sound SideSpeck Zone
Grass flats on the Mississippi Sound side. Speck country.

Salinity check first — after rain, Pascagoula pushes freshwater in. Under 12 PPT? Move east or wait.

Popping cork + live shrimp, incoming tide, 2–3 ft over grass. Best: first 2 hours after sunup.
🔵Channel Edges — Midday Heat MoveSummer
When FLIR shows flats above 82°F, fish push to channel edges 3–5° cooler at depth. Carolina rig on the bottom. Reds and big specks both stack here when shallow water dies.
